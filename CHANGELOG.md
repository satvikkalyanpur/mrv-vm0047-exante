# Changelog

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-15)

- Planting date moved from June 2025 to June 2026, the season in which the proof of concept was built. "So far" now covers 5 months (0.25 credits accrued, none issued). The ten-year total is unchanged (43.1); verifications fall in 2031 and 2036.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-14)
- The plot: boundary figure with side lengths, and a satellite map in the notebook.
- So far: month-by-month table and chart from planting to the as-of date, with CPCRI field stages.
- Reality check: modelled carbon per palm against measured stands (Das et al. 2021; Bhoomika et al. 2023).
- Observed record: the proof-of-concept NDVI measurement, plus a no-login collector for rainfall (NASA POWER) and NDVI (MODIS 250 m), and an optional Earth Engine cell for monthly Sentinel-2 NDVI.
- Establishment facts (planting season, seedling age, pre-bearing period) cited from CPCRI.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-14)
- VM0047 rule engine, ten-year ex-ante estimate, sensitivity, leakage alternative, hash-chain ledger.
